Freshman Orientation Directives

General Stacks Code

01
Loan Limits & Duration
First-year undergraduates may hold up to 3 core curriculum books for 14 calendar days. General fiction and journals are restricted to 7-day borrowing.
02
Reserve Desk Overnights
Volumes bearing red reserve stickers cannot leave reading halls prior to 20:00. Overnight checkouts must be returned before 09:00 the following morning.
03
Quiet Zones & Study Bays
Level 2 and Level 3 East are designated silent study zones. Cell phones, audio speaker reproduction, and collaborative discussion must be kept to ground floor pods.
